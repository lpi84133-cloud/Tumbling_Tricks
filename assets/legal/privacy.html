Privacy Policy

Tumbling Tricks · last updated 7 August 2026

The short version: Tumbling Tricks keeps everything you enter on your own device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. This page is bundled inside the app, which is why it opens with no connection.

What the app stores

Everything you create is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your device:

  • Acts, run orders, blocks and beats
  • Your trick library, including tricks you add and how you rate them
  • Checklists, stage plots and notes
  • Rehearsal sessions: date, length, focus and how the run felt
  • Your performer profile: stage name, discipline, home venue, description
  • Your preferences, including reminder times

The performer photo

If you add a photo, it is copied into the app's own storage and reduced in size. The copy is re-encoded, which removes the metadata the original carried, including any location information. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the photo in Backstage removes the file from your device.

Camera and photo library access

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only at the moment you choose to add a performer photo, and only for that purpose. If you decline, every other feature continues to work. You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or profiling
  • Nothing sent to any server — the app has no networking code for your content
  • No data sold or shared with third parties, because none leaves the device

Notifications

Rehearsal reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. Nothing about your acts leaves the device to produce them, and they are only requested if you turn a reminder on.

Backups

Because your data lives in the app's storage, it is included in your own iCloud or Finder backup of the device if you have one enabled. That backup is under Apple's control and this app's terms do not apply to it. You can also export your own copy from Backstage; where that file goes afterwards is up to you.

Deleting your data

Backstage has an option that erases every act, trick, note, rehearsal and photo the app holds. Deleting the app from your device removes all of it as well.

Children

Tumbling Tricks is a planning tool for performers and is not directed at children. It collects nothing about anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version ships inside a new release of the app and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@tumblingtricks.app.
