Support & FAQ

Tumbling Tricks · bundled with the app, works offline

Email support@tumblingtricks.app with what you were doing and what happened. Mentioning your iOS version and device model helps.

Getting started

  1. Open an act on the Stage Console and give it a target running time.
  2. Build the run order: blocks first, then drop tricks into them as beats.
  3. Work through the checklist and stage plot as things get confirmed.
  4. Log each rehearsal. Readiness rises as real preparation is recorded.

What does the readiness percentage actually measure?

Six things, weighted: blocks that contain material (15%), planned time against your target (10%), beats you have timed and choreographed (20%), checklist progress (25%), stage plot lines confirmed (15%), and rehearsals logged together with how those runs felt (15%). Opening the app changes none of them.

Why is my act at 100% but the checklist looks short?

Readiness measures what you have chosen to track. If an act only has four checks, clearing all four completes that signal. Add the checks the act really needs and the figure will reflect the fuller picture.

Can I use it without a connection?

Yes — every feature works offline, including this page. There is no server involved at any point.

Where is my data kept?

In the app's own private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy in Backstage for the detail.

How do I move my acts to a new phone?

Restore the new device from your iCloud or Finder backup, which includes the app's data. You can also export a copy from Backstage and keep it wherever you like.

A trick in my run order disappeared from the library.

Archiving a trick hides it from the library but leaves it in any run order it was already part of. Clear the mastery filters, or look in the archive, to find it again.

Rehearsal reminders are not arriving.

Check that the reminder is switched on in Backstage, and that notifications for Tumbling Tricks are allowed in the iOS Settings app. Reminders are scheduled locally, so they arrive whether or not you have a connection.

Can I delete everything and start again?

Yes. Backstage has an option that erases every act, trick, note, rehearsal and photo. It cannot be undone.

Why is the app portrait-only?

Run orders and checklists are long vertical lists, and a single orientation keeps them readable one-handed between runs. The launch screen supports both orientations so the app opens correctly however you are holding the device.
